## 2. Scenario / Playability Layers

Group letter tier and playability are independent.

### Group Tier

- S — true national phenomenon
- A — large major
- B — very strong chika / medium major
- C — strong chika / quasi-major
- D — professional chika
- E — small chika
- F — extremely small / data often impractical

Tier is based on scenario opening date, not future peak.

### Playability Layer

1. Recommended
2. Playable
3. Selectable
4. Background

Definitions:

- Recommended — strong data, distinctive challenge, actively highlighted
- Playable — suitable for a full campaign with usable data
- Selectable — systems function, but completeness is not guaranteed
- Background — AI/world context only

## 3. Core Producer Principle

The player is normally a professional producer under organizational constraints, not an omnipotent owner.

Different scenarios can represent very different authority structures:

- strong agency control
- project-company producer authority
- mature group-system internal allocation
- independent producer with almost no agency hierarchy

The game should model these differences rather than force every group through one generic agency template.

---

## 4. Time as Resource

No separate action-point currency.

The game uses semi-real-time / time-as-resource.

Player chooses an action, the game resolves it and advances by the action duration.

Examples:

- member interview
- rehearsal observation
- staff meeting
- training
- travel
- live appearance
- rest

The player cannot do everything personally.

Staff delegation is therefore a core management mechanic.

---

## 5. Attribute System V2

All 0–20.

### Physical
- agility
- natural_fitness
- stamina

### Appearance
- cute
- pretty

### Performance
- pitch
- tone
- breath
- rhythm
- power
- stage_presence

### Communication / Creative
- wit
- humor
- talking
- teamwork
- fashion
- creativity

Long-term abilities should change slowly.

Do not collapse them into simplistic single `sing` / `dance` ratings.

Important principles:

- `power` does not directly belong in vocal technical score
- `stamina` affects endurance/fatigue more than raw dance quality
- `stage_presence` acts at final expression/performance layer
- requirements above 17 should be rare; 18+ is elite headroom

---

## 6. Work Familiarity

Separate long-term ability from short-term mastery.

### Individual Work Familiarity

Per member, per song/work/part.

### Formation Familiarity

Per song + specific lineup/formation.

A member may be technically strong but unfamiliar with a work.

A group may contain individually well-prepared members but still be weak in a newly changed formation.

### Learning Rate

Derived rather than stored as a new personality stat.

- Vocal learning rate: dominated by `wit`, `stage_presence`, plus vocal fundamentals
- Dance learning rate: dominated by `wit`, `stage_presence`, plus dance fundamentals

This allows real cases where someone with strong musical foundation learns choreography unusually fast without changing her long-term dance attributes overnight.

---

## 7. Traits / Evidence

Traits represent persistent tendencies, desires and habits, not ability.

Examples:

- Center Aspiration
- Actress Aspiration
- Solo Aspiration
- Producer Mindset
- Variety Lover
- Idol Otaku
- Perfectionist
- Fan-Service Lover
- SNS Enthusiast
- Fashion-Conscious
- Conflict-Averse

Pure newcomers may start with no traits.

Traits use hidden, reversible evidence rather than permanent truth labels.

---

## 8. Knowledge Model

The member panel displays **staff-team collective estimate**, not omniscient truth.

Information layers:

1. true attribute
2. staff-team estimate shown on panel
3. individual staff report/opinion
4. player's own inference

These are intentionally separate.

Staff reports do not automatically narrow the panel estimate.

Knowledge should converge slower than Football Manager scouting, especially for:

- teamwork
- natural_fitness
- stage_presence
- wit
- creativity

Own-group members can eventually reach very accurate/true base attributes through long-term work.

External members remain fuzzier.

---

## 9. Fatigue / Current Ability

Player/self-character can display exact fatigue and exact effective attributes after fatigue modifiers.

Other idols:

- fatigue is fuzzy
- base attributes may be fuzzy
- fatigue-adjusted effective attributes are also fuzzy

Directional change markers can be more reliable than exact values.

---

## 10. Direct Interaction

Direct member interaction is a persistent limited-time system.

It supports:

- better targeted knowledge
- trait evidence
- trust/relationship
- role discussion
- career-goal discussion
- dissatisfaction detection
- crisis warning signs

It is never a simple stat-up button.

---

## 11. Live System

Live is primarily an immersive presentation layer, not a deep tactical minigame.

Player choices matter mostly before the live:

- lineup
- center
- part distribution
- training
- familiarity
- formation
- condition/fatigue

Viewing modes:

- Highlight — about 30 seconds per song, limited formation changes
- Skip / Instant Result

Audience quality is deliberately coarse. The system should not pretend to produce precise live-quality truth.

Detailed interpretation comes afterward through imperfect staff reports.

---

## 12. Historical Simulation Principle

> Historical convergence, not historical enforcement.

Past facts are fixed up to scenario start.

After player takeover:

- historical future becomes a soft prior
- major opportunities can remain hard calendar anchors
- conditional events only occur if relevant people/conditions exist
- player may change outcomes
- agency constraints, resources, schedules and member qualities naturally pull outcomes toward plausible historical neighborhoods

## 14.5 にっぽんワチャチャ — Special Challenge

Independent Producer Mode.

No meaningful agency hierarchy.

Initial organization can be effectively:

- Producer 1
- later Assistant 1

Most specialist work is outsourced or ad hoc.

Producer has modest industry resources and enough live-house/event contacts to make the campaign playable.

Initial roster can be largely specified.

Adding members remains possible but is difficult and costly.

Core challenge:

- extremely low-cost operations
- producer time overload
- cash flow
- live booking
- member communication
- outsourced training/content
- pandemic survival

The player may choose whether to publicly commit to the historical “3-year Budokan” vision.

Possible vision states:

- public hard commitment
- internal long-term goal
- conservative alternative target
- no major promise, survival first

Cost-cutting options may include delayed pay / arrears, with real consequences:

- morale loss
- trust loss
- dissatisfaction
- graduation risk
- contract conflict

This should be modeled as payable/arrears state, not a one-click morale penalty.

Long-term arc can eventually reach a committed Budokan project if the player chooses.

## 16.1 Last Scene — Core Concept

This scenario demonstrates mature-system talent allocation rather than mass-audition discovery.

Use the HEROINES Summer Audition 2025 as the new-candidate pool.

Default fixed member at project start:

- 天羽しおり

Other historically likely/strong candidates appear via staff recommendation and internal organizational knowledge rather than being hard-locked.

Examples include:

- 歌奏おんぷ
- 古賀みれい
- candidates recently observed in the 2025 iLiFE! audition such as 佑月いちか
- HEROINES trainees / SCHOOL members
- current or recently graduating internal members
- external experienced idols
- new Summer Audition applicants

## 16.2 Staff Recommended Contact List

Staff generates a prioritized contact list.

Possible statuses:

- Available
- Interested
- In Discussion
- Reserved by Another Project
- Agency Coordination Required
- Unavailable

The list changes over time.

Some people may be reserved by another HEROINES project.

Internal transparency does not mean the player owns the talent pool.

## 16.3 Contact / Invitation / Agency Coordination

For high-interest historical candidates, once the player directly contacts and invites them, personal acceptance can be very likely.

But organizational approval may still be required.

Possible flow:

`Contact → Interest Confirmed → Invitation → Member Accepts → Agency Coordination → Approved/Rejected`

For already-graduating or available members, coordination is easier.

For active members of another group, approval can be difficult and may require:

- current-group needs
- schedule conflicts
- strategic value
- member willingness
- management approval

## 16.4 Experienced Members Before Final Camp

Players may finalize experienced members before the final Summer Audition camp.

Benefits:

- early personal training
- deeper knowledge
- more work familiarity
- more chemistry development
- ability to help evaluate newcomers

Confirmed experienced members can join final audition training as:

- peer mentor
- peer evaluator
- temporary training leader

They can partially substitute for staff in observing:

- teamwork
- learning speed
- rehearsal habits
- communication
- pressure response

Their opinions remain subjective and separate from staff-team panel estimates.

## 16.5 Summer Audition Flow

Public recruitment occurs through the 2025 HEROINES Summer Audition structure.

The player can spend July–September:

- contacting experienced members
- reviewing previous iLiFE! audition records
- meeting HEROINES trainees/SCHOOL members
- arranging light training for already-confirmed members
- leaving some roster slots open for final audition candidates

Final historical anchor:

**2025-10-04 to 10-06 — final camp**

After final camp, the game does not force an immediate roster submission.

## 16.6 Flexible Final Roster Submission

Persistent action:

**Submit Final Roster to Agency**

The player decides when enough information has been gathered.

Tradeoff:

Early submission:

- longer individual training
- more formation familiarity
- more chemistry
- earlier branding/work preparation

Late submission:

- more information
- more candidates
- more internal coordination time
- but compressed training/debut preparation

## 16.7 Flexible Debut Timing

HEROINES has dense live activity, so the group should not be locked to one historical debut date.

Once minimum readiness is met, the player can request a debut slot.

Possible opportunities:

- smaller internal event
- HEROINES FES
- larger event
- dedicated debut event

Early debut:

- more real-stage familiarity and fan growth
- less preparation
- higher risk of rough performance

Late debut:

- stronger preparation
- less live experience and slower fan growth

## 16.8 Support Member System

Later historical crises make support-member mechanics important.

Flow:

`Need Identified → Contact Candidate → Confirm Interest → Agency Application → Approval → Support Assignment`

Support member rules:

- original group's training/live schedule has priority
- player cannot alter original-group obligations
- only a small number of support-team training slots are available
- this limits work/formation familiarity

Later options:

- persuade support member to transfer permanently
- request extension
- negotiate end of support

Permanent transfer requires member willingness and agency approval.

## 16.9 Historical Crisis Events

Later scandals/contract issues should be conditional historical events.

If relevant members are selected, real historical risk patterns may emerge.

But events are not forced simply because history says they happened.

Direct interaction, staff warnings and management decisions may allow earlier detection or altered outcomes.
