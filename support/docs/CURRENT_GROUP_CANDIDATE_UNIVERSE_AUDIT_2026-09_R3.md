# Current Japanese Female Idol Candidate-Universe Audit — 2026-09 R3

Evaluation date: **2026-09-09**. Current-market research only. This supplements R1/R2 and formalizes the inclusion boundary for voice-actor idol units.

## Scope rule: voice-actor idol units

Include groups that operate **continuously under their own group identity as an idol / music unit**, even when all members are voice actors and anime tie-ins are a major part of their work. The group must have its own artist identity, releases, tours, fan club / live product and continuity beyond one specific fictional franchise.

Examples to **include**: `i☆Ris`, `22/7`, `TrySail`, `DIALOGUE+`.

Exclude groups whose primary identity is a **fictional anime / game / multimedia-IP character project**, where the real-world lineup principally performs as the cast of that IP rather than as an independent idol group. Example: `Love Live!` school-idol units. Similar franchise-cast projects should be excluded by default unless they demonstrably develop an independent group identity outside the IP.

This boundary is about the real-world act, not whether members are voice actors and not whether songs are anime tie-ins.

Method remains `Live / Music / Brand -> Overall`, structurally prioritizing Live, with route-specific Brand/Music evidence used where the normal underground-idol festival gate is irrelevant.

## Rating notes

### i☆Ris

Current Live is materially above C+. The 2026 tour is not a one-off arena rental: multiple Zepp/PIT/hall dates sold out at **¥9,200** normal pricing, and the two Tachikawa Stage Garden shows also sold out. That supports at least B- Live, with B as a candidate if rolling-52-week attributable ticket gross confirms the expected aggregate. Keep Overall at B- for now because current Music is much smaller than the B-tier idol groups and the future Yokohama Arena should not be counted before completion.

### TrySail

TrySail is the strongest clearly in-scope voice-actor unit found in this pass. Two Budokan shows plus a high-price national hall/arena route and Pacifico Yokohama repeat products put Live in B territory. Current physical chart peaks are moderate relative to conventional idols, but streaming is strong and the independent artist brand is durable. Overall B is appropriate without needing to compare it to Love Live!-type franchise-cast projects, which are outside the universe.

### DIALOGUE+

LINE CUBE SHIBUYA selling out all price classes at ¥8,800 normal / ¥14,000 goods / ¥18,000 premium is clean paid-demand evidence. Combined with O-EAST and KT Zepp normal-price touring, current Live is C+ rather than C-. Music remains C because recent physical chart peaks are around #21-22 rather than tens of thousands of idol-style event sales. Overall C+.

## Exclusion examples

Do not add these merely because they perform real concerts:

- Love Live! franchise groups / school-idol units
- game/anime character units whose real-world activity is fundamentally the cast performance arm of the IP
- one-off seiyuu formations assembled for a title/event

The deciding question is: **would the real-world group still be a coherent artist/idol entity independent of one fictional franchise?** If yes, audit it; if no, exclude it.
