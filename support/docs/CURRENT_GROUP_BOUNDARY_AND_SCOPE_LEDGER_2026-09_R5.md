## Provisional C- rule

For ordinary live-idol routes, current C- is granted conservatively:

1. a valid current TIF HOT/Main-type floor may support C- directly; otherwise
2. trailing-12-month own-live business must be **clearly stronger than NANIMONO**, using ticket gross as the primary signal and total attendance / average or representative attendance as secondary signals.

A single large venue, a single sell-out, a low-price large show, or festival visibility alone does not establish C-. Low-price tickets are not manually double-discounted if ticket gross is already the primary measure, but they reduce gross naturally and weaken the interpretation of a nominal sell-out as evidence of paid-demand density.

Later passes will replace these provisional judgments with per-live records.

## Interpretation

This pass intentionally compresses many "large venue but uncertain paid density" acts back into D+. The purpose is to keep C- as a meaningful threshold rather than a venue-name threshold.

Current examples now separate cleanly:

- **TENRIN = C-**: repeated stronger demand and venue progression, not one peak.
- **NANIMONO = D+ anchor**: high-frequency/low-price expansion model.
- **Ringwanderung / CYNHN / fishbowl / 手羽先 / iON! / アンスリューム = D+** until per-live gross or attendance proves a clearly stronger 12-month business than NANIMONO.

## TIF note

TIF participation alone is not the floor. SKY, SMILE, HEAT, greeting, opening, radio exercise, special/finale, or scramble-derived Main should not be treated as equivalent to an ordinary independent HOT/Main booking. The floor applies only to the comparable regular Main/HOT signal defined in the tier methodology.
