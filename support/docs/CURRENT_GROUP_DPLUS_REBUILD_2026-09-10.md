- Method: `Live / Music / Brand -> Overall`, with Live structurally strongest but no mechanical averaging.

- **Groups demonstrably stronger overall than MEGAFON should remain D+.**

- **MORE STAR = C- floor:** `Live D+ / Music C / Brand C -> Overall C-`.
- Therefore Overall D+ legitimately contains heterogeneous profiles; subratings do not need to equal the Overall.
