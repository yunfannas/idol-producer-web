# Current Japanese Female Idol Tier Table — 2026-09

Working current-market calibration snapshot. This is **not** Scenario 6 data and must not be back-propagated into the 2025-07-05 scenario.

Scope: 50 currently active Japanese female-idol groups at C- or above used for market calibration. K-pop / survival-pop routes and business models too far outside the intended baseline are excluded.

Hidden dimensions follow `GROUP_TIER_EVALUATION.md`:

`Live / Music / Brand -> Overall`

For presentation and boundary comparison, the dimensions are ordered by current importance:

1. **Live** — primary structural anchor
2. **Music** — secondary market-consumption anchor
3. **Brand** — tertiary confirmation / tie-breaker

This is a priority ordering, not a fixed arithmetic weighting formula. Overall remains a structural judgment. Same-tier ordering below is therefore sorted primarily by Live, then Music, then Brand; it is a calibration aid rather than a formal public ranking.

## Interpretation notes

- **Live is the primary ordering signal.** Within the same Overall tier, a group with stronger repeatable paid drawing power is generally placed first even when another group has stronger Music.
- **Music is second.** It separates groups with similar Live by durable recorded-music consumption, combining Physical and Streaming.
- **Brand is third.** Industry position, external-work level and public recognition matter, but Brand alone should not outrank materially stronger current paid Live or Music.
- A one-step weaker Live can still coexist with a higher Overall when both Music and Brand strongly support the higher tier; 高嶺のなでしこ is the current C+ example.
- Conversely, strong Live alone does not automatically raise Overall when Music and Brand remain materially weaker; Jams Collection / Appare! / yosugala are current C examples.

## Data-quality note

Music values are current calibration estimates. Physical values are generally easier to anchor through rolling 52-week Oricon/Billboard sales. Streaming data quality varies: audited all-platform annual totals are not public for every group, so Spotify monthly listeners and song-level/chart evidence are sometimes used as a documented proxy. Re-review a Music value when better hard streaming totals become available.
