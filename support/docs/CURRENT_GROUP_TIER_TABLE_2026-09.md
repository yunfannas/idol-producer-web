## Interpretation notes

- Same-tier ordering is primarily a calibration aid, not a formal public ranking.
- Live remains the first structural anchor, but a single large peak or annual gross accumulated through unusually cheap/high-frequency events cannot independently force promotion.
- Current-year regular TIF HOT/Main remains a C- scene-floor signal for ordinary live-idol routes, while non-TIF/major ecosystems can reach the same tier through other structural evidence.
- Music and Brand can support or limit Overall, but neither should be treated as an automatic arithmetic offset to Live.
