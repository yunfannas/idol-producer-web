## Key changes from the previous current-market draft

- `Sales` is replaced by unified `Music`.
- 高嶺のなでしこ Brand is corrected from B- to **C+**; its C+ Overall is now `C+ / C / C+`.
- きゅるりんってしてみて moves from C to **C+** after its completed normal-price 2026 hall-tour scale is recognized; current profile `C+ / C+ / C`.
- ≒JOY remains **B-**, reflecting completed 2026 Budokan/K-Arena-scale live evidence and very strong physical consumption.
- Streaming-heavy acts can have Music above their physical tier; physical-heavy acts are not penalized merely for weak streaming.

## Data-quality note

Music values are current calibration estimates. Physical values are generally easier to anchor through rolling 52-week Oricon/Billboard sales. Streaming data quality varies: audited all-platform annual totals are not public for every group, so Spotify monthly listeners and song-level/chart evidence are sometimes used as a documented proxy. Re-review a Music value when better hard streaming totals become available.
