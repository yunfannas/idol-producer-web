## Interpretation notes

### Onephony
Toyosu PIT has a standing capacity of 3,103, so the 2025-12 tour final is a genuine large-venue attempt. However, the ticket ladder included a ¥1,000 invitation zone alongside high-priced VIP products. Without recovered attendance / sell-through, we should not infer a 3,000-person paid product. Current rating therefore remains D+ with an upward trend.

### Sweet Alley
Zepp DiverCity standing capacity is 2,473, but the 2026-09-02 own live used ¥1,000 A and invitation zones. This is a textbook case where venue ceiling and low-price expansion must be separated. Until annual gross / paid attendance is recovered, D+ is safer than C-.

### lyrical school
The act has durable cultural/brand value and a long history, but current own-live scale is small. In 2025 the official site shows regular MARZ events, a free one-man, and a ¥1,000 one-man product. This is not a C- Live business under the current framework.

### 美味しい曖昧
The group sits in the stronger music-oriented festival pool, and current standard pricing is healthier than many cheap-ticket acts. Still, the completed 2026 anniversary product is Shibuya Pleasure Pleasure scale rather than O-EAST/Zepp, while the 2026-11 O-WEST one-man is future at the evaluation date. Keep D+.

## Boundary consequence

This pass reinforces the stricter calibration:
- `Toyosu PIT scheduled/completed` alone does not imply C-.
- `Zepp` alone does not imply C- when cheap/invitation zones are material and sell-through is unknown.
- A group without HOT/Main should either demonstrate clearly superior 12-month own-live gross / attendance versus NANIMONO, or remain D+ provisionally.
