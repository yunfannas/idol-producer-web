# Current Japanese Female Idol Tier Research Tracker — 2026-09

Working research table for **current-market** calibration. This is not Scenario 6 data and must not be back-propagated to the 2025-07-05 scenario.

## Current rules

Overall priority: **Live > Music > Brand**.

Live is evaluated from repeatable normal-price paid demand, not nominal venue capacity. Record both raw attendance and standard-price equivalent attendance (SPEA) when possible.

### SPEA

Reference standard ticket price: **¥5,000**.

For each ticket class:

- below ¥5,000: weight approximately `price / 5000`
- ¥5,000: weight 1.00
- premium tickets above ¥5,000: modest bonus only, capped around 1.20
- free / near-free distribution should contribute little to Live demand

Premium / front-area tickets are commonly a minority of total capacity and often sell out before general tickets. Until exact allocation is known, use these working assumptions only as fallback estimates:

- true VIP / 最前: ~5% of capacity
- single 前方 / premium section: ~12–15%
- VIP + 前方 combined: ~15–20%
- >25% premium share requires explicit evidence

A premium sell-out proves core demand, not whole-venue demand.

### Working Live scale

| Live | Repeatable SPEA / structural meaning |
|---|---|
| D+ | ~500–800; strong professional live-idol, below stable 1k paid level |
| C- | ~800–1,200; stable ~1k-class effective paid demand |
| C | ~1,800–2,500; stable Zepp-class / multi-Zepp normal-price product |
| C+ | ~3,000–5,000 repeatedly; clearly beyond ordinary Zepp ceiling |
| B- | ~5,000–8,000 repeatedly |
| B | ~8,000–12,000+ repeatedly |

There is intentionally a gap between bands: promotion should reflect a structural step, not a tiny numerical change.

### TIF gate for ordinary live-idol route

For ordinary TIF/live-idol ecosystem groups, **no regular TIF Main Stage / HOT Stage in either of the most recent two years normally means no C-**.

Exceptions:

1. other dimensions or independent paid-Live evidence are clearly strong enough; or
2. the group operates in a materially different ecosystem where TIF placement is not a valid gate.

Special企画,争奪winner, collaboration-only, finale-only appearances are not equivalent to a regular Main Stage slot.

## Research procedure per group

For each group, collect one representative recent tour / one-man pricing structure first. Because pricing is usually stable within a tour and often follows a group-level tradition, do not redundantly collect every stop unless pricing changes.

Record:

1. evaluation date
2. event / tour name
3. venue and usable capacity
4. ticket classes and prices
5. estimated allocation by ticket class
6. sales status by class (SOLD OUT / 残少 / available / same-day ticket)
7. estimated units sold by class
8. raw attendance if reported
9. SPEA
10. whether the event is ordinary, anniversary, graduation, free/discounted, fan-club, or otherwise exceptional
11. city / region and whether similar demand repeats elsewhere
12. confidence grade

Use actual ticket allocation where known. Otherwise use the premium-share fallback only as an estimate and mark it clearly.
