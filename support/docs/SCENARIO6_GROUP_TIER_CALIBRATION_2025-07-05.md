# Scenario 6 Japanese Female Idol Tier Calibration — 2025-07-05

Historical market calibration for **Scenario 6 opening date: 2025-07-05**.

- **S6 rating cutoff:** 2025-07-05.
- **Dimensions:** `Live / Music / Brand -> Overall`.
- **Priority:** Live > Music > Brand, but Overall is a structural judgment rather than a fixed arithmetic average.
- **Trend is retrospective for S6:** it uses the actual market state through **2026-08-31** as a post-hoc trajectory label and does **not** change the 2025-07-05 S6 rating.
- `↑↑` = rapid rise, `↑` = rise, `-` = broadly flat, `↓` = decline, `↓↓` = large decline.
- `STOP` is reserved for groups that ended, or had already announced an end, within roughly one year after the S6 opening. Later announcements do not retroactively create an S6 `STOP` label.
- The main table contains every currently confirmed S6 group at **Overall C- or above**. The count is an output of the calibration, not a fixed target.

## Evidence / historical-universe policy

- Every group that already existed and had not formally ended by **2025-07-05** is eligible, even if it later disbanded, reorganized, or declined.
- Later evidence can validate the trajectory (`Trend`) but must not be backfilled into S6 `Live / Music / Brand / Overall`.
- TIF2024 and TIF2025 can be used as near-opening scene-position evidence. A normal independent TIF HOT / UP-T HOT appearance is a strong C-level Brand signal; special winner/collaboration/finale mechanisms are not treated the same way.
- Own-live economics and repeatability remain primary for Live. A one-off large venue is a ceiling signal, not automatically the baseline tier.

- **高嶺のなでしこ = C / C / C / C, Trend `-`** at S6. Later sales/brand growth is not backfilled.

- **≒JOY = B-, Trend `↑↑`**.
- **Jams Collection = C-**, not C. The 2024 Budokan is treated as a one-time peak rather than a new repeatable baseline.
- **RAIN TREE = Live D+ / Music C- / Brand C -> Overall C-, Trend `↑↑`**. This is a deliberate strong-project initial-positioning case.
- **AVAM, yosugala, ZOCX, 東京女子流** are confirmed inside the S6 C- universe after boundary review.
- **fav me, #ババババンビ, UtaGe!** remain D+ at S6 and therefore are not in this table.
- **TEAM SHACHI** and **東京女子流** are `STOP` because their end-of-activity timing falls within the S6 retrospective window. **超ときめき♡宣伝部** is not `STOP` for S6 because its later announced ending lies outside that roughly one-year window.

## Boundary notes

The S6 C- band is intentionally heterogeneous. Examples include:

- Live-led: `yosugala`, `FES☆TIVE`.
- Music/Brand-supported: `ZOCX`, `SWEET STEADY`, `ロージークロニクル`.
- Legacy Brand with still-real paid Live: `東京女子流`.
- Initial-positioning floor: `RAIN TREE`.

The table should therefore not be reduced to a mechanical majority vote among the three dimensions.
