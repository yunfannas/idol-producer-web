# Scenario 6 Japanese Female Idol Tier Calibration — 2025-07-05

Historical market snapshot for **Scenario 6 opening date: 2025-07-05**. This file is intentionally separate from the 2026-09 current-market calibration and must not inherit later growth automatically.

Dimensions use the same current standard:

`Live / Music / Brand -> Overall`

Priority is **Live > Music > Brand**, with Overall determined structurally rather than by a fixed arithmetic formula. Music combines physical CD sales and streaming. Brand measures group-level industry position, external-work level and public recognition; streaming is not counted again in Brand.

## Evidence / cutoff policy

- Primary state is what was knowable or economically established by **2025-07-05**.
- TIF2024 is contemporaneous evidence for scene position entering S6.
- TIF2025 stage allocation may be used as a **near-future validation check** because the user explicitly requested it, but the August 2025 performance itself is not treated as already-realized S6 opening-date business.
- Festival placement is a consistency check, not a fourth scoring dimension. TIF2024 officially used HOT STAGE and HEAT GARAGE as its top two stages; TIF2025 similarly describes UP-T HOT STAGE and HEAT GARAGE as its leading pair.
- The existing S6 festival validation pilot remains useful at the lower boundary: its IDORISE 2025 sample found all sampled C+/C groups reached O-EAST, 75% of sampled C- groups did, and none of the sampled D groups did. Agency/promoter confounding remains explicit.
