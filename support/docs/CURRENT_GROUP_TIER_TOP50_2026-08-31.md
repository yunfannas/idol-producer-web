## Brand calibration note

For Current Brand, a normal current-year **TIF HOT/Main Stage** appearance is treated as an immediate **Brand C floor**. If a group does not make HOT/Main in the current year, historical HOT/Main alone does not preserve Brand C; another current C-level signal such as sustained major activity, media/public reach, or equivalent industry position is required.

This rule concerns the annual TIF-stage certification signal specifically. Other durable Brand evidence can still support a higher Brand independently.
