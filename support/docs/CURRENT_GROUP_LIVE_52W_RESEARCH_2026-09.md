# Current Japanese Female Idol Live — Rolling 52-Week Research Pass (2026-09)

Working research pass for current-market calibration. Evaluation date: 2026-09-09. Approximate rolling window: 2025-09-10 through 2026-09-09. This is **not** Scenario 6 data.

## Method

Primary Live signal is **rolling 52-week attributable ticket gross**.

Secondary signals are:
- paid admissions over the same 52-week window
- representative / median own-show gross
- P75 own-show gross
- largest repeatable own-show gross
- peak / special-event gross, stored separately

Count at 100%: one-man, official group tour, anniversary/birthday/graduation shows billed as the group's own live.

Joint two-man / co-bill: count only an attributable share when evidence exists.

Do not allocate ordinary taiban/festival/TIF gate receipts to the group. Free-live ticket gross is zero. Benefit-event / cheki revenue is excluded from Live.

Ticket gross should use actual ticket prices without capping premium-ticket value. When class sales are unpublished, estimate class allocation and sell-through explicitly and preserve a range.

The annual-gross estimate below is intentionally shown as a range because exact ticket-class sell-through is often unpublished. Venue name alone never determines gross.

## Evidence corrections from the second pass

### 1. きゅるして was under-estimated in the first annual pass

The 2026 hall tour itself consists of nine regional hall dates before the two Makuhari Event Hall additions. Public promoter / official-facing pages explicitly mark multiple regional dates SOLD OUT, including Niigata, Hiroshima, Takamatsu and Kobe; contemporary official promotional material listed the regional hall run as SOLD OUT.

Known venue capacities already show that this is not a small hall circuit: examples include Niterra Forest Hall 2,291, Niigata Kenmin Kaikan 1,730, Sunport Hall Takamatsu 1,500 and Kobe Kokusai Hall 2,112. With general ¥6,600, student ¥3,900 and goods ¥12,500 tickets, the nine regional dates alone plausibly generate well above ¥100m gross before Makuhari.

Makuhari then uses much higher pricing: ¥17,000 front-goods / ¥15,000 goods / ¥8,800 general / ¥5,000 student. Because exact configured capacity and class allocation are not yet recovered, keep a range, but the previous ¥160m lower bound for the whole 52-week period was too low.

### 2. 高嶺のなでしこ tour frequency was also under-counted

The prior table treated each venue date roughly as one show. The official tour schedule states a first and second performance on the Zepp/PIT dates. Therefore Sapporo, Osaka Bayside, Nagoya, DiverCity, Fukuoka and Sendai PIT represent **12 performances**, not six, before the Forum A final.

This substantially raises rolling annual ticket business even if individual shows are not all full. At ¥7,300 normal price, a 1,500–2,000 paid audience already implies roughly ¥11m–14.6m gross before the ¥12,000 camera-area tickets. Sendai provides a useful hard sales-status point: first performance SOLD OUT, second `残りわずか`.

This creates an important profile distinction:

- annual ticket business: clearly C+ scale
- representative per-show audience breadth: closer to C
- peak events: C+ / B- gross possible

So 高猫 should not be described as weak annual Live business merely because a typical individual show is around ~2k. The argument against Live C+ is about **per-show audience breadth and ceiling**, not annual revenue.

### 3. NANIMONO remains the key high-frequency / low-yield case

Two completed tours alone contain 29 own paid shows. This is enough to create non-trivial annual gross even though typical inventory is only ¥5k / ¥3k / ¥1.5–2k / ¥0.5–1k. The 2026 tour explicitly lowered the rear tier to ¥500 for its first 11 shows.

Annual Gross should therefore prevent NANIMONO from being treated like an ordinary low-frequency D+ act, but representative-show gross still prevents promotion to ordinary-route C- without stronger independent evidence.

### 4. INUWASI moves upward when full tour frequency is counted

Two major band-set tour cycles inside the rolling window contribute at least seven substantial own shows: EX Theater, Nagoya ReNY, BananaHall, Zepp DiverCity, BIGCAT, Diamond Hall and LINE CUBE. Prices are commercially healthy relative to many live-idol peers, with premium tiers around ¥10k–15k and ordinary tiers around ¥3.5k–4.5k.

This makes INUWASI the cleanest current test of the C promotion line. Additional own/birthday dates can plausibly move the rolling total into the lower C gross band, but its representative regional operating scale is still smaller than a clean C baseline.

## C-promotion-line implication

This five-group pass does **not** provide evidence for lowering the working C annual-gross band below roughly ¥80m.

The observed structure is instead:

- ~¥30m–50m can support a stable C- when pricing/frequency/scene position are healthy (Task, MBT lower range, yosugala current window)
- ~¥45m–80m is upper C- territory and can contain groups with clear C-class peaks (AVAM, MBT, INUWASI lower range)
- ~¥80m+ becomes a meaningful C annual-business signal, but only if representative/median own-show gross also clears D+/low-C- structure
- high-frequency small-show accumulation or one exceptional peak must not independently trigger C

So the current working anchors remain defensible:

- D+: below ~¥40m, or D+-scale median despite higher annual frequency
- C-: ~¥40m–80m
- C: ~¥80m–150m
- C+: ~¥150m–300m

The main unresolved boundary sample is now **INUWASI**, not Task or yosugala.

## Working Live interpretation

Do not assign Live from annual gross alone. Use a structural combination:

1. **52w attributable ticket gross** — primary measure of the group's actual Live business
2. **representative / median own-show gross** — determines the normal operating scale
3. **paid admissions** — measures audience breadth independently of premium pricing
4. **P75 / largest repeatable gross** — detects stable ceiling
5. **peak special-event gross** — records upside but cannot define normal scale by itself

A high-frequency group can therefore have annual business one tier above its representative show scale without automatically receiving the higher final Live tier.
