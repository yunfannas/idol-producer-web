## Key interpretation changes

1. **衛星とカラテア should remain in the current C- universe unless direct 2026 draw evidence proves decline.** It was C- at S6 and still has O-EAST-class own-live products plus national-route continuity. Its absence from the first current table is best treated as an omission rather than an intentional demotion.
2. **ExWHYZ clearly belongs in the current ranking at C, with a final-year flag.** A 15-city normal-price tour in 2025 followed by a 2026 final national tour ending at Zepp DiverCity is stronger than ordinary C- live-idol structure. Later dissolution does not affect inclusion at the 2026-09-09 snapshot because the group is still active.
3. **AMEFURASSHI is not a current-ranking candidate because it already ended in March 2026.** Keep its tier in historical/game data; do not include it in the September current universe.
4. **豆柴の大群 has unusually strong Brand relative to current Live.** Public recognition from the TV/WACK/Avex route does not by itself elevate current Overall; current own-live scale still looks D+/C- boundary.
5. **#Mooove! remains D+.** A Zepp anniversary peak does not override the current small-venue, low-price repeatable product.
6. **Palette Parade is a genuine C- boundary case.** The completed LINE CUBE anniversary is important, but ¥2k general pricing means actual attendance and ticket-class mix are required before promotion.
