# Current Group Tier Re-run — D+ through B- (2026-09)

Working second-pass re-evaluation for the current Japanese female-idol market. Evaluation date: 2026-09-09. This is **not** Scenario 6 data.

## Current decision rule

Overall is judged from three hidden market dimensions:

`Live / Music / Brand -> Overall`

Priority is **Live > Music > Brand**. This is not a fixed weighted average.

### Live
Primary signal is **rolling 52-week attributable own-live ticket gross**. Secondary checks are representative/median own-show gross, paid attendance breadth, repeatability and geographic breadth. Member birthday SP lives count as own paid live. Ordinary taiban/festival receipts are not separately modeled for lower live-idol groups; their effect is treated as already expressed through sustainable own-live demand and scene/Brand evidence.

Working annual Live-gross anchors after the current sample pass:

- D+: below roughly ¥40m, or a D+-scale median show even if frequency pushes annual gross higher
- C-: roughly ¥40–80m
- C: roughly ¥80–150m
- C+: roughly ¥150–300m
- B-: roughly ¥300–500m, normally also requiring repeated ¥25–40m-class own shows / stable ~5k-class paid product

These are calibration bands, not hard cuts. A high-frequency small-show model does not automatically inherit the tier implied by annual gross alone.

### Music
Music combines Physical + Streaming. Physical benefit-event/multi-buy sales remain real commercial consumption and are not discounted. Streaming does not affect Brand.

### Brand
Brand is group-level industry position + external-work level + public recognition. Agency resources, member fame and streaming alone do not count as group Brand.

## C- lower-bound anchors

- **Task have Fun** — standard live-idol / legacy-route C- tail. Repeatable normal-price touring is enough to keep Live at C- even without a current-year HOT slot.
- **ロージークロニクル** — HP/major-route C- tail. Music and organized paid touring provide a different but valid C- structure.
- **UtaGe!** — current-scene-floor C-. Cheap ordinary monetization would otherwise be borderline, but a 2026 regular TIF HOT/Main slot supplies the explicit current scene floor.
- **ラフ×ラフ** — D+ immediately below the survival line. Strong anniversary peak and Brand C do not substitute for repeatable C- Live business.
- **NANIMONO** — D+ head via the opposite structure: unusually high annual frequency/gross but D+-scale median shows and aggressive low-price expansion.

This means the C- / D+ boundary is deliberately multidimensional. A group does not become C- merely because one peak show, annual frequency, or Brand reaches C-class evidence.

## Main revisions from the previous 50-group table

1. **高嶺のなでしこ Live C -> C+**: annual ticket business matters; >¥150m 52w gross is enough for C+ when generated by a repeatable tour product, even if individual shows are often only ~2k attendance.
2. **Jams Collection Live C+ -> C-; Overall C -> C-**: previous venue/peak-driven Live estimate was too high. Birthday SPs raise annual gross substantially, but representative ticket gross remains C- and total is likely around the C-/C boundary.
3. **Appare! Live C+ -> C-; Overall C -> C-**: a 15-show tour with ¥0 general tickets cannot be valued like a normal-price tour.
4. **yosugala Live C+ -> C-; Overall C -> C-**: current cutoff catches a low-frequency period before the next national tour.
5. **FES☆TIVE / Devil ANTHEM. Live C -> C-; Overall C -> C-**: high activity and large special venues were overstating repeatable ticket business.
6. **INUWASI Live C -> C-**: becomes the standard upper-C- Live anchor rather than a C anchor.
7. **SWEET STEADY Live C -> B-; Overall C -> B-**: arena attendance and ordinary pricing are materially beyond C+ single-show scale.
8. **可憐なアイボリー Overall C- -> D+** and **GILTY×GILTY Overall C- -> D+**: prior C- rows were stale.
9. **≒JOY leaves the band upward**: Live is now B rather than B-.
10. **ラフ×ラフ Live C- -> D+; Overall C- -> D+**: KT Zepp is treated as a peak rather than repeatable baseline; Brand C cannot independently maintain C-.
11. **Task have Fun / ロージークロニクル retained at C-** and are now explicit lower-edge anchors for standard live-idol and HP/major-route structures respectively.
