## Interpretation

### AsIs
AsIs is the strongest upward boundary case in this pass. The 2025 comparable structure was a first national tour ending at Shinagawa Intercity Hall with mixed ¥10k/¥7k/¥4.5k/¥1k pricing. The 2026 route reaches Zepp DiverCity, with S already sold out pre-show. This supports `trend ↑↑` from the S6 baseline and a current **C- provisional** label, but current Live should not be frozen until the 2026-09-09 Zepp attendance / sell-through is known.

### Palette Parade
Palette Parade has repeatedly selected venues larger than its ordinary paid base: 2025 Zepp DiverCity and 2026 LINE CUBE SHIBUYA, while preserving cheap ordinary / early-entry inventory. This makes it an excellent boundary anchor. Keep **C- provisional**, not locked C-, until actual audience breadth or class sell-through is recovered.

### SITUASION / ジエメイ
Both are examples where alternative-scene visibility can look stronger than commercial own-live demand. Their Brand can sit at C- scene level while Overall remains D+. This is useful as a counterexample against promoting acts solely because they share festival bills with C- groups.

### Mirror,Mirror / アンスリューム
Both appear to have been omitted from the old current universe. Mirror,Mirror now has repeated LIQUIDROOM-to-O-EAST own-live progression; アンスリューム has an O-EAST one-man around the S6 cutoff and a 2026 Zepp DiverCity own one-man. Because both use relatively cheap general/rear tickets, neither should exceed C- without stronger gross evidence.
