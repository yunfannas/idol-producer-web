# Current Japanese Female Idol Groups — C- or Above Master (2026-09)

Evaluation date: **2026-09-09**. Current-market ranking scope only. This consolidates the old 50-group table plus confirmed additions from candidate-universe audits R1-R4. It is **not Scenario 6 data**.

## Scope

- Main table contains current, ranking-eligible Japanese female idol groups with working **Overall C- or above**.
- Independent voice-actor artist units such as i☆Ris, TrySail, DIALOGUE+ and 22/7 may receive game tiers but are **excluded from this real-world ranking**.
- Fictional-IP cast projects such as Love Live! units are outside the ranking universe.
- Groups that are still active at the evaluation date but have officially announced termination remain in the snapshot and receive `STOP`.
- `Trend` is forward-looking and does **not** modify current Overall. Values: `↑↑ / ↑ / - / ↓ / ↓↓ / STOP`.
- Default `-` means no sufficiently strong directional evidence has yet been established; it does not mean the group is literally static.

`Live / Music / Brand -> Overall`

Priority remains **Live > Music > Brand**, with structural judgment rather than arithmetic averaging.
